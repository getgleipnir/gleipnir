Verify agent action records

Check a Gleipnir bundle (bundle.zip) or a single-action proof (proof.zip) that a vendor sent you: were the records changed after they were written?

Stays on your computer. The file is checked in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, and the page makes no network requests. You can save this page and use it offline.

Stricter checks
The key id the vendor publishes, for example in its trust center. Fails if the records are signed with another key.
The head from an earlier bundle of the same chain. Fails if that record is missing or different (history cut short or rewritten). Use the form seq:sha256:hash, as shown under Head in an earlier result.
Require independent anchors on every segment
Fails segments without a trusted timestamp, or timestamped later than this: catches history rebuilt after the fact. One day is 86400. Enter a number of seconds, for example 86400.
A time with a timezone. Fails if the records stop earlier (cut short). Use a time with a timezone, for example 2026-10-14T18:00:00Z.

Valid means no record was edited, removed, reordered or inserted after it was written, and the anchors check out. It does not mean the agent's decisions were right, and it cannot show actions that were never recorded: compare the ids in the records with your own systems (gleipnir-verify reconcile).

Same checks as the gleipnir-verify command line tool (Python and Node), using @getgleipnir/verify.
