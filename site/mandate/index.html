Sign an agent mandate

Your company wants an AI agent to make bank transfers. Check exactly what it may pay, to whom and until when. If it is right, sign with your passkey. Your bank will refuse every payment outside this mandate.

Nothing is uploaded and the page makes no network requests. Your passkey signs on this device; you copy the signed mandate and hand it back to whoever asked (or into your bank's app).

Passkeys are not available here. Open this page over https (or on localhost) in a current browser.

From gleipnir mandate issue, the agent's request_mandate, or the link you were sent.

You

Passkey details
The domain your passkey belongs to: this page's host unless your bank says otherwise.

Gleipnir mandates (spec 0.2). The passkey signs SHA-256("gleipnir/mandate/v0.2\n" + mandate hash), so this signature is valid for this mandate and nothing else. The bank checks the signature, the origin and the relying party, and who you are, from its own KYB records.
