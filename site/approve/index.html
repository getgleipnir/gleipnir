Approve an agent action

An AI agent wants to take an action that needs your approval. Check what it is, then approve it with your passkey. Your approval covers this exact action and nothing else.

Nothing is uploaded and the page makes no network requests. Your passkey signs the action on this device; you copy the result and send it back to whoever asked.

Passkeys are not available here. Open this page over https (or on localhost) in a current browser.
